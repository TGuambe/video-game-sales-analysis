# Insights

## 1. Nintendo Titles Dominate the Highest Global Sales
Nintendo titles dominate the top 10 games by recorded global sales, with several of the highest-selling titles appearing on platforms such as the Wii, DS, Game Boy and NES.
This suggests a strong presence of Nintendo titles and platforms among the dataset's top global performers.

## 2. Regional Sales Differ
Initial average recorded sales across all games were approximately:

| Region | Average Sales |
|---|---:|
| North America | 0.39 million |
| Europe | 0.34 million |
| Japan | 0.20 million |
| Other | 0.83 million |

The relatively high average for Other regions should be interpreted carefully. An average can be strongly affected by a small number of unusually successful games.

## 3. North America Was the Most Common Dominant Regional Market
When comparing the four regional sales values for each game, North America was the largest recorded regional market for approximately 9,867 games, with average dominant-region sales of about 0.39 million.
Japan was dominant for approximately 4,080 games, averaging about 0.20 million.
Europe was dominant for approximately 2,322 games, averaging about 0.34 million.
Other regions were dominant for approximately 76 games, but those games averaged about 0.83 million in Other sales. Because this is based on a much smaller group, the average is especially sensitive to high-performing titles.

## 4. 2008 Had the Highest Total Global Sales
2008 recorded the highest total global sales at approximately **671.9 million units**.
This makes 2008 an important year for further investigation into the games, genres, platforms and publishers that contributed to the total.

## 5. Regional Sales Peaks Occurred in Different Years

| Region | Highest-Selling Year | Total Sales |
|---|---:|---:|
| Global | 2008 | 671.90 million |
| North America | 2008 | 348.69 million |
| Europe | 2009 | 187.94 million |
| Japan | 2006 | 73.74 million |
| Other | 2008 | 81.42 million |

The regional markets therefore did not all reach their highest recorded sales at the same time. North America and Other peaked in 2008, Japan peaked in 2006, and Europe peaked in 2009.

## 6. 2008 Contains Several Major Commercial Releases
The 2008 releases include major titles associated with franchises such as Mario Kart, Super Smash Bros., Grand Theft Auto, Pokémon, Call of Duty and Gears of War.
Major platforms represented include the Nintendo Wii, Xbox 360, PlayStation 3, Nintendo DS and PSP.

These observations provide useful areas for further analysis, but the presence of successful titles does not by itself prove that they caused the 2008 sales peak. 

## 7. Action, Sports and Shooter Were Among the Strongest-Selling Genres
Analysing the genres, there is a string indication that Action, Sports and Shooter were among the highest-selling genres by total recorded global sales, with each exceeding approximately **1 billion units**.
Role-Playing, Platform, Miscellaneous, Racing and Fighting also recorded substantial sales totals, exceeding approximately **500 million units** per genre in the analysis.


## Summary
The initial EDA suggests that Nintendo titles are strongly represented among the highest-selling games, 2008 was the dataset's global sales peak, and regional markets reached their peaks in different years.

The genre analysis also indicates strong overall sales for Action, Sports and Shooter titles. Regional analysis shows that North America was the most common dominant market for individual games, while the relatively high average for Other regions was based on a much smaller group of games and should therefore be interpreted cautiously.

## Conclusion
Global and regional video-game sales vary considerably by year, region, genre and platform. Nintendo titles dominate the highest-selling games, 2008 represents the dataset's global sales peak, and regional markets reach their peaks at different times. Genre-level analysis further shows that Action, Sports and Shooter titles account for substantial portions of recorded global sales.
