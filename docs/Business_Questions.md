## Platform
32. Which platform recorded the highest total global sales?

> **Important:** The dataset does not contain profit or cost information. Therefore, the publisher analysis measures **sales**, not profit.

## Analytical Scope
The project focuses on sales volume, regional differences, time trends, genre performance, platform performance, publisher performance, and critic/user reception. 
The analysis identifies patterns in the data rather than proving causal relationships.
